## Decision value of the next experiment

The next planned question was whether deliverable-first sequencing improves delivery effort at equivalent quality (METH-018). Its concrete risk is an unsupported efficiency claim or adoption recommendation. The current delivery makes neither: it preserves optional guidance and explicitly reports missing comparative evidence.

| Possible comparison outcome | Could support after adequate measurement/review | Effect on the current documentary delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Better effort with equivalent quality | A narrowly scoped efficacy/adoption review. | No change to current coverage; would require its own evidence and decision. |
| No benefit, worse effort or lower quality | Retain optional status, narrow guidance or reconsider that tested use. | Current package already claims no demonstrated efficiency benefit. |
| Incomplete/confounded outcome | No efficacy conclusion. | Current optional/pending classification remains. |

**Disposition: DEFER execution for this scope; comparison NOT RUN.** The assessment is complete, while efficacy remains unvalidated. A new study needs independent executions, matched tasks, stable quality grading and effort accounting; authoring another unexecuted harness would not supply those observations. Its additional cost has no proportionate decision value for completing the current documentary transfer. This is a scoped application of GOV-VAL-001, not a new waiver, cancellation of all future validation or proof of inefficacy. No acceptance threshold has been lowered.

Before reopening, identify the actual sequencing/adoption decision and accountable owner. Freeze matched representative inputs, the single changed condition, environment/provider, output units, quality/failure criteria, assignment/order and independent-execution eligibility. Specify elapsed/active effort, retries, rework and reviewer effort; separate preparation/overhead, do not equate bytes with tokens/cost, preserve failures and confounders, and define stop/acceptance rules before running. Confirm that plausible outcomes can change that decision and that the resources/scope are authorized. No fixed participant count, new subscription or multi-agent requirement is imposed by this checkpoint.
