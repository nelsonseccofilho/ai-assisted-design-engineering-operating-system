# ADR-0006 — Require parity before UX evolution

**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Status:** `ACCEPTED`  
**Scope:** `FRAMEWORK`

## Context

AI systems tend to optimize and simplify. During reconstruction, this can unintentionally change approved rules, content, states, hierarchy, or interaction behavior.

## Decision

When rebuilding approved work, reach sufficient functional and visual parity before opening a separate UX Evolution phase.

## Why

Reconstruction and improvement are different tasks and should not be conflated.

## Impact

Approved product intent is preserved before optimization begins.
