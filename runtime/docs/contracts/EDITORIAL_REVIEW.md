# Context-sensitive editorial review

**Ownership:** SHARED_CONTRACT. **Maturity:** configurable method; no locale's project convention is imposed universally.

Inspect the whole scene, audience, domain meaning, source decision and connected states before text replacement. User-facing terminology may be more specific than an internal object name, but changing a label does not rename the backend entity or change its permissions. Distinguish request, confirmation, cancellation, rejection, unlink and destructive deletion by actual actor/effect; do not infer equivalence from similar words or icons.

For numeric labels, define the locale and editorial policy first. Review zero, one and multiple entities separately from absence messages, category titles, fractions, capacity/cycle indicators, decimals and units. A locale plural category does not necessarily mean numerical equality to one. Verify the chosen implementation and current language reference rather than assuming a library's plural category matches the product convention.

Protect human-reviewed compound indicators until numerator/denominator semantics, surrounding copy and approval context are recovered. Do not perform blanket singular/plural or domain-noun substitutions across a product. Similar-looking phrases can express different models.

For an authorized copy change, preserve styles, semantic bindings, opacity, text geometry, overrides and relevant states. Reconcile corrections against the existing comment/task record and owner history. A text fix does not itself resolve a native comment thread or establish stakeholder acceptance. Report the exact patterns and scope checked; a scan pass is not exhaustive human editorial QA.
